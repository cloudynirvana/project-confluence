# Medical Disclaimer

## This Is a Research Framework

Project Confluence is a **computational research tool** designed for scientific exploration of dynamical systems approaches to cancer metabolism and closed-loop control. It is:

- **NOT** a medical device
- **NOT** a clinical decision support system
- **NOT** a diagnostic, prognostic, or therapeutic tool
- **NOT** approved by the FDA, EMA, or any regulatory body
- **NOT** a claim of clinical admissibility, disease eradication, or a treatment path

Connectome controllers (including the optional 166,700-unit sparse net and “protein” / antibody effector channels) are **in-silico control signals**. They do not synthesize therapeutics, express genes, or constitute a biologic manufacturing or dosing system.

**Simulated metrics are research scores on an ODE.** Tumor-burden, resistance, fusion-allele, junction-neoantigen, immune-competence, surveillance, antibody-readiness, and therapy-channel numbers in this repository are not disease eradication, not clinical benefit, and not a path to a clinical outcome. Fusion-protein terms are inspired by chimeric-mRNA biology (BCR–ABL / EML4–ALK / NTRK-class); they are not a clinical fusion assay. Antibody / BiTE / IgG channels are PK/PD class references, not prescribed regimens and not ribosomal synthesis in Drosophila neurons.

Disease-class labels (`benign`, `malignant`, `occult`, `dormant`, `terminal`) are **state-signature modes** for computational discrimination. They are not clinical stage, TNM, or histopathology diagnoses.

RECIST 1.1-like / H-band surrogate (CTCAE-like) / Kaplan–Meier / log-rank / Cox outputs in this repository are an **in-silico endpoint mapping / computational–clinical translation layer**. They are simulated mappings from ODE burden and host-health H for scientific scrutiny. True CR is reserved for burden near the detection floor. Infusion U is unitless and normalized, not a mg/kg regimen. They are not a clinical trial, not RECIST/CTCAE adjudication, not FDA/EMA readiness, and not a Phase II result. Controller F figures use an F-256 proxy unless explicitly run at 166,700 units.

## No Medical Advice

Nothing in this repository constitutes medical advice. The computational models, drug optimization outputs, and complexity profiles are **theoretical constructs** for research purposes only.
