CONFLUENCE: An Evidence-Gated Dynamical Framework for Integrating Oncology Knowledge, Molecular Observations and Adaptive Cancer-State Modeling

Branch feat/onco-adapter-p0 · PR #9. Page date 19 September 2026. Statistics keep their original estimate year.

Scope (Gate 0). CONFLUENCE is a computational research framework. Current results are computational unless an external experiment is cited. Not a clinical decision-support system. Not a universal cure. Simulated trajectories are not patient outcomes.

Abstract

Cancer research now produces knowledge graphs, multi-omic assays and dynamical simulators in parallel. The failure mode is collapsing those layers. This study asks whether a provenance-controlled pipeline can keep the layers apart while still allowing testable predictions. Scientific success is a staged chain — traceability, mathematical validity, identifiability, out-of-sample prediction, experimental falsification — not disease eradication. PR #9 is architectural evidence for the gates, not therapeutic efficacy.

Why the study is necessary

GLOBOCAN 2024 estimates, published 2026: about 20.6 million diagnoses and 9.8 million deaths (EVID-001). Breast cancer about 2.43 million new cases (EVID-002). WHO: many cancers can be cured if found early and treated well; access is uneven (EVID-003). Nigeria GLOBOCAN 2022 profile: 127,763 new cases, 79,542 deaths, breast 32,278 — 2022 estimates, not 2026 incidence (EVID-004).

Tumours evolve and adapt under therapy (EVID-006). Multi-omics increase resolution without automatically producing a causal model. Mathematical oncology supplies in-silico laboratories (EVID-007). Adaptive therapy treats treatment as a process under selection (EVID-008). The gap is connecting knowledge to dynamical hypotheses without dropping provenance.

Evidence for the problem

TNBC is a test case because NCI describes it as roughly 15% of breast cancers, typically faster-growing and more recurrent, and heterogeneous (EVID-005). That is not a CONFLUENCE parameter. This branch adds a read-only OnCo adapter with nine tests that refuse knowledge-as-parameter and do not edit CancerODE (EVID-009).

Knowledge ≠ Evidence ≠ Causal mechanism ≠ Parameter ≠ Prediction

legacy gene→parameter map ≠ identified parameter · structured representation ≠ biological observation

Problem statement

How can heterogeneous oncology knowledge and molecular observations be incorporated into a cancer dynamical model without collapsing evidence, mechanism, parameterisation and prediction into unsupported assumptions?

Knowledge → Evidence → Hypothesis → Mechanism → Parameter → Prediction → Experiment → New evidence

Every arrow is a failure point. A page may motivate a hypothesis; it must not become Theta.

Significance of the study

Scientific

Treat cancer as dynamic and adaptive rather than as independent targets (EVID-006).

Methodological

Evidence gates. OnCo confidence is not P(H). Idea maturity is not evidence level. Failed hypotheses stay visible.

Computational

Auditable objects. PR #9 is the current artifact (EVID-009).

Translational potential — not demonstrated benefit

Models can compare hypotheses before a wet experiment. This page does not claim patient benefit. Translation waits for Gates 6–8.

Future research

Attach genomic, single-cell, spatial, PK/PD and controllers around a frozen biological model.

Success protocol

Not a universal cure. Sequential and falsifiable:

Gate 0Research-only scope.
Gate 1Provenance: source, URI, date, population.
Gate 2Evidence class named.
Gate 3Mechanism: intervention, state, sign, context, falsifier.
Gate 4Parameter: model id, symbol, units, estimator, uncertainty, identifiability. Unidentified ≠ established.
Gate 5Prediction labelled as computational.
Gate 6Held-out / external / baseline validation.
Gate 7An experiment that could show the prediction is wrong.
Gate 8Translation only after biological and clinical validation this repository does not claim.

Traceability → mathematical validity → identifiability → out-of-sample prediction → experimental falsification.
